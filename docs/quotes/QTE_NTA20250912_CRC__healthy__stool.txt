Summary: The primary objective of this study is to obtain stool from subjects with 
colorectal cancer (“CRC”) and healthy subjects. 
 
Service Type: Prospective collection 
 
Cohorts:   50 total subjects will be collected in the following two Cohorts: 
• Cohort 1: CRC (25 subjects).  Subjects will be collected either pre-
therapy (preferred) or within 2 weeks of neoadjuvant therapy start.  
Stool must be collected at least 7 days post colonoscopy / biopsy and 
before resection surgery. 
• Cohort 2: Healthy (25 subjects).  Subjects must be ≥45 years old and 
do not need to be matched in any way with the Cohort 1 subjects. 
 
It is acceptable that subjects enrolled in this study also enroll (or be 
enrolled) in another Natera study with MT Group. 
 
Biospecimens:  Stool will be collected in a Natera kit following instructions described in 
“CRC and healthy collections_ Kit insert.”  Subjects will collect and 
process their stool samples on Mondays through Thursdays then call 
FedEx for pickup at their location (porch pickup is ok).  Collection kits, 
shipping boxes, and shipping labels will be provided by Natera.  Any 
changes to the approach described herein will be mutually agreed by 
Natera and MT Group. 
 
 Biospecimens for each Cohort will be collected as follows: 
• Cohort 1.  One or two stools per subject will be collected.  Subjects 
will collect and FedEx the first stool at least 7 days post colonoscopy 
/ biopsy and the second stool a day or up to a few days later (but still 
before the resection surgery).  If the second stool is not available for 
any reason (e.g. subject does not want to provide, runs out of time), a 
single stool per subject is acceptable. Note that MT Group sites will 
encourage multiple stools to be provided by each subject but cannot 
guarantee this from any subject. 
• Cohort 2.  Two stools per subject will be collected with the second 
stool collected and FedExed one to two days after the first.  
 
Clinical Data:  Both Cohorts: age, sex, race, ethnicity, height, weight, self-reported co-
morbidities, and self-reported medications.  Cohort 1 only: stage, grade, 
TNM, date of colonoscopy, and date of surgery..  If subjects have already 
been enrolled or will be enrolled in another Natera study with MT 
Group, the other MT study number will be noted in the clinical data. 
Shipping & Handling:    Biospecimens will be shipped by the enrolled subjects via FedEx Priority 
Overnight using Natera shipping labels. 
   
Study Timeline: A study kick off call will be scheduled ~1 week after 3-4 weeks after MT 
Group and Natera finalize the SOP / align on all study parameters.  MT 
Group expects to complete enrollment 10-12 months after study 
initiation.  
 
Biospecimen Costs: $2,000 / Cohort 1 subject stool 
 $1,500 / Cohort 2 subject stool 
 
Shipping Kits to Sites: $50 / subject stool 
 
Total Study Cost: $180,000 (estimating that 100% of subjects provide two stools)
