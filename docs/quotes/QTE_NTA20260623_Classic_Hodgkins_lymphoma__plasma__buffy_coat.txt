Quote Number: QTE NTA20260623 Classic Hodgkin’s lymphoma – plasma & buffy __________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________
Study Objectives: This study will prospectively collect biospecimens from subjects with 
classic Hodgkin’s lymphoma
Size: 20 subjects
I/E Criteria: Inclusions
 Diagnosed with classic Hodgkin’s lymphoma
 Collected before any treatment
 Collected in the Ukraine
No other inclusions or exclusions
Biospecimens: Whole blood will be collected in 3 x 10 mL Streck tubes and double spun 
to plasma using MT Group standard processes (1 plasma aliquot per 
Streck tube).  Whole blood will also be collected 1 x 10 mL K2EDTA 
tube and double spun for a buffy coat using MT Group standard 
processes. 
Clinical Data: MT Group and Natera will mutually agree on all clinical data to be 
provided for each subject but at a minimum, sample & subject IDs, 
collection dates & times, basic demographics, diagnosis, and a path 
report or summary will be provided. 
Timeline: A study kick-off call will be scheduled within ~1 week of receipt of a 
Natera purchase order.  Site initiation will begin ~4 weeks after the kick-
off call and alignment on all study details.  We expect to complete 
enrollment 6-8 months after site initiation with all subjects delivered ~1 
month after enrollment completion.  It is understood that Natera will not 
terminate this study early if MT Group is enrolling subjects as expected.
Shipping & Handling: Biospecimens will be batch shipped on dry ice to a United States location 
designated by Natera using FedEx Priority Overnight or equivalent.  It is 
understood that if freight charges increase dramatically during the study 
execution, shipping & handling costs may also be increased. 
Biospecimen & Data Costs: $1,650 / subject
Shipping and Handling: $150 / subject
Total Study Costs: $36,000
