characterized, whole blood specimens from US sites to evaluate 
biomarkers associated with cancer for diagnostic assay development.

Inclusions & Exclusions: Inclusions 
• Participant is ≥18 years of age 
• Participant understands the study procedures and is able to provide 
informed consent to participate in the study and authorization for 
release of relevant protected health information to the study 
Investigator. 
• Participant has an untreated primary malignancy or has a suspected 
primary malignancy of bladder or ovarian cancer (Cohort #1 only).   
• Participant has been diagnosed by a treating physician for benign 
disease or been evaluated for benign or malignant disease (Cohort #2 
only) 
• Pathology report for cancer subjects and all required clinical data 
must be available 
     
Exclusions 
• Prior or concurrent cancer diagnosis  
• Other prior or concurrent cancer diagnosis defined as: 
o Any previous cancer diagnosis within the past 5 years (with 
the exceptions of basal cell or squamous cell skin cancers); 
OR  
o Recurrence of the same primary cancer within any 
timeframe; OR  
o Concurrent diagnosis of multiple primary cancers  
• Chemotherapy and/or radiation therapy within 5 years prior to 
enrollment/sample collection.  
• Any treatment for the primary malignancy or sites of metastases. 
Subject may not have started neoadjuvant chemotherapy, 
neoadjuvant radiation therapy, immunotherapy, or other treatment 
and/or resection surgery / excisional biopsy prior to blood sample 
collection.  It is acceptable that Cohort 1 subjects have received 
therapies for pre-cancerous conditions although details about these 
therapies (especially immunomodulary ones) must be captured with 
the clinical data.  
• Less than 3 days between fine needle aspiration (FNA) of target 
pathology and blood collection.  
• Less than 7 days between biopsy (other than FNA) of target 
pathology and blood collection.   
• IV contrast (e.g. CT and MRI) within 1 day [or 24 hours] of blood 
collection.  
• Individual has a condition the Investigator believes would interfere 
with his or her ability to provide informed consent, comply with the 
study protocol, which might confound the interpretation of the study 
results or put the person at undue risk.  
• Known to be pregnant  
• Participated or currently participating in a clinical research study in 
which an experimental medication has been administered in the last 
30 days   
 
Biospecimens:  Whole blood collected in 4 x 10mL Streck tubes (30 mL minimum blood 
volume). Streck tubes will be provided by Natera. 
 
At Enrollment Clinical Data:  Demographics, co-morbidities, medications, and other clinical data will 
be collected as shown below.  Pathology reports needed to document 
disease diagnosis will be provided.   A CBC count taken within the last 
12 months will be provided if available. 
 
Demographics 
• Age 
• Height 
• Weight 
• Smoking status 
• Sex 
• Family history of cancer 
• Ethnic and racial background 
 
Comorbidities to note 
• Cardiovascular disease  
• Type II Diabetes  
• Type I Diabetes  
• IBD (Ulcerative colitis or Crohn's)  
• Rheumatoid Arthritis  
• Pancreatitis  
• Liver disease (fatty liver/liver cirrhosis, NASH, NAFLD)  
• Thyroid disease  
• Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD)  
• Other autoimmune diseases (e.g. multiple sclerosis, lupus, etc.)  
• Germline cancer mutation carrier  
• Other 
 
Medication categories 
• Aspirin, Ibuprofen (such as Advil, Motrin), Naproxen (such as

• Cholesterol lowering drugs, either “statin” or other drugs   
• Steroids taken orally (e.g., Prednisone, Decadron, Medrol)

• Vitamin D supplements

• GLP-1 inhibitors
