Study Objectives: This study will prospectively collect biospecimens from subjects with a 
“clean” colonoscopy report and subjects with advanced adenoma.
Size: Subjects collected in the following two Cohorts:
 Cohort 1.  Colonoscopy-confirmed clean subjects (120 draws of 4 x 
10 mL whole blood per draw as further described below)
 Cohort 2:  Advanced adenoma subjects (120 subjects)
Cohort 1 I/E Criteria: Inclusions
 Confirmed via standard of care screening colonoscopy to not have 
advanced adenoma (“AA”) or colorectal cancer (“CRC”)
 Collected either 0-30 days before colonoscopy and before any 
colonoscopy-related anesthesia or 7-30 days after the colonoscopy
 Collected in Turkey
Exclusions
 Subjects recommended for symptomatic-driven colonoscopy (i.e. 
blood in stool)
 Subjects with an incomplete bowel preparation
 Subjects with a known familial history of CRC
 Subjects not providing consent
No additional inclusions or exclusions
Cohort 2 I/E Criteria: Inclusions
 Subjects with AA, as defined as having one or more of the 
pathological findings shown below:
o adenoma with carcinoma in situ or high grade dysplasia, any 
size (i.e. CRC stage 0) adenoma
o villous growth pattern (>25%)
o any adenoma >1.0 cm in size
o serrated lesion >1.0 cm in size 
o traditional serrated adenoma (TSA), any size
 Collected either 0-30 days before the colonoscopy and before the 
colonoscopy-related anesthesia or 7-30 days after the colonoscopy 
but before all AA-associated polyps are removed
 Collected in Turkey

Exclusions
 Subject not providing consent

Biospecimens: Whole blood will be collected in 10 mL Streck tubes and processed to 
plasma following the protocol described in “Plasma Isolations for 
Vendor (5).doc” or as otherwise mutually agreed. All Streck tubes will 
be provided by Natera.  One plasma aliquot of 3.5 mL – 5 mL per Streck 
tube.  MT Group sites can only draw 4 x 10 mL tubes’ worth of blood 
per draw and each draw has the same site cost.
It is acceptable for MT Group sites to draw Cohort 1 subjects at multiple 
times as long as the inclusion / exclusion criteria are met. It is also 
acceptable for Cohort 1 subjects to have been previously collected for 
another Natera study.
Clinical Data: MT Group and Natera will mutually agree on all clinical data to be 
provided for each subject but at a minimum, sample & subject IDs, 
collection dates & times, and basic demographics will be provided.  A 
translated colonoscopy report will also be provided as will a translated 
path report or summary (Cohort 2 only).  Clinical data will be provided 
in an Excel spreadsheet generally following the format of “Example AA 
data needed_21Jul2026.xls”).
Timeline: A study kick-off call will be scheduled within ~1 week of receipt of a 
Natera purchase order.  Site initiation will begin ~4 weeks after the kick-
off call and alignment on all study details.  We expect to complete 
enrollment ~2 months after site initiation with all subjects delivered ~1 
month after enrollment completion.  It is understood that Natera will not 
terminate this study early if MT Group is enrolling subjects as expected.
Shipping & Handling: Plasma will be batch shipped on dry ice to a United States location 
designated by Natera using FedEx Priority Overnight or equivalent.  It is 
understood that if freight charges increase dramatically during the study 
execution, shipping & handling costs may also be increased.
