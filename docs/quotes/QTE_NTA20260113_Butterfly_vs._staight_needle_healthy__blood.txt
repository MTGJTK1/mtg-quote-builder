Summary: Prospective collection of samples for the evaluation of different needles’ 
effect on blood biomarkers. 
 
Service Type: Prospective collection 
 
Cohorts:   75 self- reported healthy subjects 
 
Inclusions / Exclusions: Inclusions 
• Collected in the United States 
• Self-reported as healthy 
• Understand the study procedures and can provide written informed 
consent to participate in the study and authorization for release of 
relevant protected health information to the study investigator 
 
Exclusions 
• Not diagnosed with cancer  
• Any prior history or treatment for any kind of malignancy 
(exception: subjects who have undergone surgical removal of skin 
squamous or basal cell cancer may be enrolled provided the 
procedure was completed at least 12 months prior to the date of 
provision of informed consent for the study) 
• Physical trauma or surgical procedure that required hospitalization 
(e.g. disruption of tissue, surgery, organ transplant, blood product 
transfusion) within 4 weeks of the blood draw 
• May have been diagnosed with the following comorbidities (these 
co-morbidities are not excluded) 
o Cardiovascular disease 
o Hypertension 
o Type II Diabetes 
o Type I Diabetes 
o IBD (Ulcerative colitis or Crohn’s) 
o Rheumatoid Arthritis 
o Pancreatitis 
o Liver disease (fatty liver / liver cirrhosis, NASH, NAFLD) 
o Thyroid disease 
o Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) 
o Other autoimmune diseases (e.g. multiple sclerosis, lupus) 
o Germline cancer mutation carrier 
 
Biospecimens:  Whole blood collected in four (4) Streck tubes as follows: 
• Tubes 1 and 2: drawn from one arm using a 21 gauge straight needle 
• Tubes 3 and 4: drawn from the opposite arm using a 21 gauge 
butterfly needle 
 
Natera will provide all Streck tubes / collection kits.   
 
Clinical Data:  Age, sex, arm and draw order for each tube.  Data will be provided in a 
mutually-agreed spreadsheet (no Medrio entry).   
  
Shipping & Handling:    Biospecimens will be batch shipped to Natera on ambient gel packs via 
FedEx Priority Overnight as available.   
   
Study Timeline: A study kick off call will be scheduled ~1 week after MT Group receives 
a PO.  Study initiation will occur 3-4 weeks after MT Group and Natera 
finalize the SOP / align on all study parameters and tubes are received by 
MT Group.  MT Group expects to complete enrollment ~1 month after 
study initiation.  
 
Biospecimen Costs: $1,100 / subject
