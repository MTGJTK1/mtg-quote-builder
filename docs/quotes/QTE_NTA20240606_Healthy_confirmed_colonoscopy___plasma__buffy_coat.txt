Summary: The goal of this study is to collect plasma and buffy coat from subjects 
without cancer or advanced adenoma per colonoscopy. 
 
Service Type: Retrospective 
 
Populations and Size:  20 subjects that have been determined to not have cancer or advanced 
adenoma per colonoscopy.  Collected from the Turkey site that collected 
the advanced adenoma cases provided under PUR1000018526 and 
prepared using the same process as those cases.   
 
Biospecimens:  2 biospecimens will be provided for each case: 
• 8-10 mL of double-spun Streck plasma will be provided for each 
case 
• 1-2 buffy coats (each buffy coat collected from a single Streck tube) 
will be provided for each case if available.   
 
MT Group will invoice based upon the actual samples delivered.   
 
Clinical Data Provided:  All available clinical data will be provided in an Excel spreadsheet but at 
a minimum, sample & subject IDs, collection dates & times, basic 
demographics, and diagnosis will be provided.  A translated colonoscopy 
report will also be provided.   
 
Shipping & Handling:    Plasma & buffy coat will be batch shipped on dry ice to a US-based 
Natera location via Fedex Priority Overnight. 
   
Timeline: We expect to ship the cases to Natera within 6-8 weeks of a purchase 
order for its evaluation.   
 
Biospecimens & Data: $600 / case for the plasma  
 $100 / case for buffy coat (if available)
